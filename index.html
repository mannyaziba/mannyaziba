UX Intelligence Audit — Cleva

Cleva was losing users to a feedback silence in onboarding and a structure collapse in the dashboard. Here is the diagnosis.

Loom embed — add after recording
01 Signup — Feedback. No loading state after form submission. The interface goes silent on click. Add a spinner on submit and a success confirmation within two seconds.
02 Dashboard — Structure. Every element carries equal visual weight. The eye has nowhere to land. Establish one primary action at 3× the weight of everything else on screen.
03 Dashboard — Flow. No prompted next action after onboarding completes. A single directional CTA eliminates the dead-end state entirely.
