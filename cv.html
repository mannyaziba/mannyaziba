Profile

I design the interface layer for products built by developers. My method is structural — I use a five-lens diagnostic framework to identify exactly where an interface is losing users, then resolve the root cause rather than the symptom. I have built two live design tools as proof of this method, with additional tools in active development. I do not do aesthetic design in isolation. I do interface intelligence — the discipline of making technically-sound products legible, navigable, and usable by the people they are built for.
